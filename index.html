About

Jordan Lambert

Jordan is a media specialist and freelance videographer based in Little Rock, Arkansas, building JRL Media as a full-service production studio — one project, one client, one story at a time.

A Magna Cum Laude graduate of the University of Memphis, Jordan is currently completing a Master's in Mass Communication with a focus in Public Relations at the University of Arkansas at Little Rock. Her work has supported mission-driven organizations including the Oscar Washington Jr. Educational Foundation, A Better Glynn, and Arkansas Baptist College — bringing consistent, professional media support to teams doing meaningful community work across Little Rock.
